# EngazCRM

EngazCRM is the verified direct competitor currently used for Egyptian-market comparison.

Observed positioning:

- visible market presence and a real user base;
- unit-level real-estate inventory management;
- basic phone, email, SMS, and WhatsApp engagement;
- public app-store complaints that include search and usability concerns.

CRMPro will not copy EngazCRM's live unit-inventory model. The project-level model remains a core
product decision because agencies do not consistently maintain accurate developer inventory,
availability, and pricing inside a CRM.

That decision is reinforced by direct industry input from a sales director: developer-provided
data is inconsistent and becomes stale quickly.

CRMPro's intended differentiation is:

- clearer project-level lead workflows;
- stronger usability and search;
- auditable multi-channel communication;
- outcome attribution from ad click through Closed Won;
- access to dated source price lists through Project Files without pretending that CRMPro owns a
  live, authoritative unit inventory.

# V Estate

V Estate is not currently treated as a material competitive threat.

The investigated “top CRM” ranking appeared to be self-published by the same company selling the
product rather than an independent market source, and no convincing market traction was found in
the planning review.

This is a provisional assessment, not proof that the product has no users. Re-check V Estate if
independent adoption, customer references, app usage, or other credible traction becomes visible.

---

# Positioning Guardrails

- Do not claim CRMPro provides authoritative live unit inventory.
- Do not build unit inventory merely to match a competitor's feature list.
- Prefer direct market evidence and independent customer signals over vendor-authored rankings.
- Keep competitor comparisons dated and evidence-backed.
- Emphasize operational clarity, project-level lead management, communication history, and
  measurable sales outcomes.
